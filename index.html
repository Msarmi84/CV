About

I’m a Frontend Developer and Digital Designer
with a background in Advertising and Public Relations and a passion for creating digital experiences where design and technology come together.
I’m detail-oriented, organized and curious, with a strong interest in creating digital products that are both functional and visually engaging.

Since 2021...

I’ve been working professionally in web development, building and maintaining responsive websites and applications using PHP, JavaScript, Bootstrap, HTML5 and CSS3.

Alongside development, I work on the creative and visual side of digital projects, designing web interfaces, logos and visual assets with Figma, Photoshop and Illustrator.

I enjoy being involved throughout the process — from the initial idea and visual concept to the final implementation — and I’m always looking for ways to improve my workflow and learn new technologies, including AI-assisted development with Claude. See some of my works.
